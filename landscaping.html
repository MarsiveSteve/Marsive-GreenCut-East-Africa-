Landscaping that looks good and works for your space

Good landscaping balances beauty, practicality and the environment. Marsive GreenCare East Africa designs and creates green spaces that suit how you use the land, the local climate and the time you have to look after it. We work with corporate and institutional clients, urban homes and estates, and reserve-area projects.

Who we landscape for

Corporate & institutional

Office compounds, business parks, schools and institutions that want welcoming, well-kept grounds.

Urban homes & estates

Gardens, estate common areas and compact urban plots that need to make the most of limited space.

Reserve & open areas

Green spaces on larger sites where planting, tree cover and low-impact design matter.

What landscaping with us can include

  • Planting plans for lawns, flower beds, shrubs, hedges and ground cover.
  • Tree selection and placement for shade, screening and beauty. See tree planting.
  • Lawn establishment and renovation, with ongoing lawn care.
  • Water-efficient layouts that work with irrigation.
  • Upgrades to existing gardens and compounds that need a fresh look.

Our approach

  1. Understand the space. We look at the site, soil, sun, water access and how the area is used.
  2. Agree the plan. We discuss your goals, preferences and budget before work begins.
  3. Plant and build. Plants are chosen to suit the site, with attention to water use and long-term upkeep.
  4. Care for it. We can advise on, or help with, the maintenance that keeps the space looking its best.

Principles we follow

  • Right plant, right place. Plants chosen for your altitude, sun and soil need less water and fewer replacements.
  • Local where it makes sense. Indigenous plants and trees support local wildlife and often cope better with local conditions.
  • Design for upkeep. A beautiful garden you cannot maintain will not stay beautiful.

We serve Nairobi, Kiambu, Murang'a, Nyeri, Kirinyaga, Embu. For practical ideas, read our green landscaping tips.
