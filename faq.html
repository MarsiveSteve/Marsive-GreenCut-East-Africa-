Frequently asked questions

Which areas does Marsive GreenCare East Africa serve?

We currently work in Nairobi, Kiambu, Murang'a, Nyeri, Kirinyaga and Embu counties. We are working towards serving more Kenyan counties and, later, the wider East African region. If you are outside these areas, contact us and we will let you know what is possible.

What services do you offer?

We offer landscaping and landscape design, lawn mowing and land mowing, hedge trimming, irrigation setup, tree planting, green-space development and training on sustainable practices.

Do you work with companies and institutions as well as homeowners?

Yes. We work with corporate and institutional clients, urban homes and estates, and reserve or conservancy areas.

How do I book a service or request a quote?

Use the booking form on our homepage, message us on WhatsApp, or call us. Share your location, the service you need and a few details about the space, and we will get back to you.

How much does landscaping or irrigation cost?

Cost depends on the size of the space, the design, the plants and materials chosen, and site conditions. Contact us with details of your project so we can advise and quote.

When is the best time to plant trees in Kenya?

In much of central Kenya, the start of the long rains (around March to May) and the short rains (around October to December) are the best times, because young trees get natural watering while their roots establish. With irrigation, planting can be extended outside these windows.

How often should a lawn be mowed?

It depends on the grass, the season and the look you want. During fast growth in the rains, many lawns need mowing every one to two weeks; in dry spells, less often. Avoid cutting off more than about a third of the blade at once.

Can irrigation really save water?

A well-designed system delivers water where and when plants need it, instead of watering by hand or letting water run to waste. Drip lines, correctly placed sprinklers and early-morning schedules all reduce losses to evaporation and runoff.

Do you plant indigenous trees?

We advise on species that suit your site, altitude, soil and purpose, and we encourage indigenous species where they are appropriate. Our tree planting page explains how we approach selection.

Do you offer training?

Yes. We run training on sustainable practices such as water-wise gardening, tree planting and aftercare, and green-space maintenance. Contact us with your group and goals and we will propose a programme.

Will you expand to other counties and countries?

That is our goal. We plan to grow into more Kenyan counties first and then across East Africa. Follow our updates or contact us to ask about your area.
