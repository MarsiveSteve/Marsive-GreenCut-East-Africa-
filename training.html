Sustainable Practices Training in Kenya

Practical, tailored training for teams and communities who care for land, water and green spaces.

Book a Service WhatsApp Us

Training that turns sustainable ideas into everyday practice

As well as working on green spaces, Marsive GreenCare East Africa trains people to care for them well. Our training on sustainable practices helps organisations, institutions and communities look after land, water and plants in ways that last.

Who the training is for

  • Companies and institutions with grounds, compounds or campuses to manage.
  • Schools, estate managers and property management teams.
  • Community groups, conservation and reserve teams, and farmers or gardeners who want practical skills.

Topics we can cover

We tailor each session to your group and site. Topics can include:

  • Water-wise gardening and irrigation basics
  • Selecting, planting and caring for trees
  • Lawn and green-space maintenance
  • Healthy soil, mulching and composting
  • Rainwater harvesting basics
  • Managing garden and green waste responsibly

How to arrange training

Tell us who the training is for, where you are, how many people will attend and what you want them to learn. We will propose a programme to fit. You can request it through the booking form by choosing Consultation & Training, by WhatsApp, or by phone.

Training pairs well with our landscaping, irrigation and tree planting services, so your team can keep new green spaces thriving. We are based in central Kenya and serve Nairobi, Kiambu, Murang'a, Nyeri, Kirinyaga, Embu.
