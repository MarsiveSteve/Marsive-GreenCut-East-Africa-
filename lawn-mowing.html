Lawn care that keeps your green space healthy

A well-kept lawn does more than look good. Mowing at the right height and the right time encourages dense, even growth, keeps weeds from taking over and helps grass cope with dry spells. Marsive GreenCare East Africa mows and maintains lawns and open land for corporate compounds, residential estates, institutions and private homes.

What we can help with

  • Lawn mowing for small home gardens through to large compounds.
  • Land mowing to bring overgrown plots and open spaces back under control.
  • Edging and trimming along paths, flower beds and boundaries for a clean finish.
  • Hedge trimming and shaping to keep hedges tidy and healthy.
  • Making spaces greener, from repairing bare patches to adding plants, trees and ground cover. See our landscaping and tree planting services.

Tell us whether you need a one-off visit or regular care and we will advise on what suits your space.

Lawn care tips for Kenyan conditions

  • Mow little and often. Avoid cutting off more than about a third of the grass blade at once; scalping weakens the lawn.
  • Follow the seasons. Grass grows fastest in the rains and slows in dry spells, so the mowing schedule should change with the weather.
  • Water deeply, not daily. Thorough watering early in the morning encourages deeper roots and loses less water to evaporation. See irrigation for efficient set-ups.
  • Keep mower blades sharp. Clean cuts heal faster and reduce browning at the tips.
  • Know your grass. Kikuyu grass is common across the Kenyan highlands and copes well with foot traffic. Other grasses, such as Bermuda grass, are used in warmer areas. The best routine depends on the grass you have.
