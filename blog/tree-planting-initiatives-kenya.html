Tree Planting in Kenya: How to Choose, Plant and Care for Trees

When to plant, which trees to choose, and how to help young trees survive and thrive.

Book a Service WhatsApp Us

By Marsive GreenCare East Africa · Tree planting

Planting a tree takes minutes. Helping it survive takes planning. Many young trees fail because they are the wrong species for the site, planted at the wrong time, or left without water and protection. Here is how to improve the odds.

Plant at the right time

In much of central Kenya, the best planting times are the start of the long rains (around March to May) and the short rains (around October to December). The rains water young trees naturally while their roots settle in. Outside the rains, plan to irrigate.

Choose the right species for your site

Ask what the tree is for: shade, screening, flowers, fruit, timber or habitat. Then match it to your altitude, rainfall, soil and available space. Indigenous species such as Croton megalocarpus, Cordia africana, Markhamia lutea and wild olive (Olea africana) are worth considering in many highland and mid-altitude areas, because they are adapted to local conditions and support local wildlife. Check suitability for your exact location before you buy.

Think about the tree's mature size. Large trees with strong roots should be planted well away from buildings, drains, pipes and boundary walls.

Buy healthy seedlings

Choose seedlings with a strong, straight stem, healthy leaves and a root system that fills the bag without circling tightly. Buy from a reputable nursery and plant soon after you collect them.

Plant it properly

  1. Dig a hole about twice as wide as the root ball and no deeper than it.
  2. Remove the seedling bag carefully without breaking the root ball.
  3. Set the tree upright with the root collar level with the ground.
  4. Backfill, firm the soil gently and water thoroughly.
  5. Spread mulch around the tree, keeping it clear of the trunk.

Care for it in the first years

Water regularly through dry spells, remove weeds around the base, and protect the tree from livestock, fire and damage. Stake it only if it is exposed to strong wind, and loosen ties as the trunk thickens.

Plant with a plan

Trees work best as part of the whole space. Our tree planting service helps you choose species, plant and care for them, and our landscaping team can combine trees with lawns, shrubs and ground cover.
