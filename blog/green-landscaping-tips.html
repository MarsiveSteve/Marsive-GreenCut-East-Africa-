Green Landscaping Tips for Offices, Estates and Homes

Plan, plant and maintain outdoor spaces that look good, save water and are easy to look after.

Book a Service WhatsApp Us

By Marsive GreenCare East Africa · Landscaping

Whether you manage an office compound, an estate or a family garden, a few simple principles make green spaces look better, use less water and cost less to maintain.

Start with a plan

Walk the site and note where the sun falls, where water collects, where the soil is poor and how people move through the space. Decide what each area is for: relaxing, welcoming visitors, play, shade or screening. A simple plan avoids expensive changes later.

Choose plants that suit the site

Plants that suit your altitude, sun and soil need less water and fewer replacements. Group plants with similar water needs together so you can water each area properly.

Use trees for shade and structure

Trees cool outdoor areas, frame buildings and give a space maturity. Choose species by their mature size and root habits, and keep large trees away from buildings, drains and boundary walls. Read our guide to tree planting in Kenya for more.

Save water from the start

  • Use drip irrigation for beds, shrubs and trees.
  • Water early in the morning or late in the evening.
  • Mulch beds and tree bases to keep the soil moist.
  • Reduce lawn areas where they are hard to water or rarely used, and replace them with ground cover or planting beds.

Design for easy upkeep

Choose a layout your team or household can realistically maintain. Simple, clean edges, sensible plant spacing and regular, light maintenance beat occasional big clear-ups. Our lawn care service can help keep things tidy.

Think about wildlife

Flowering plants and indigenous trees attract birds, bees and butterflies, and add life and colour to a space.
