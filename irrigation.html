Irrigation that saves water and keeps plants thriving

Watering by hand or with an unplanned hose wastes time and water. An irrigation system designed for your space delivers the right amount of water to the right plants at the right time. Marsive GreenCare East Africa sets up irrigation for lawns, gardens, planted beds and larger grounds.

What an irrigation setup can include

  • Drip irrigation for flower beds, shrubs, hedges and trees, delivering water close to the roots.
  • Sprinkler systems for lawns and larger open areas, laid out for even coverage.
  • Zoning and scheduling so lawns, beds and trees each get what they need.
  • Timers and controllers to water at the best time of day without anyone having to remember.
  • Advice on water sources, including mains supply and storage tanks, and how to use them efficiently.

Why irrigate efficiently

  • Lower water waste. Water goes where plants need it instead of running off or evaporating.
  • Healthier plants. Consistent, deep watering supports stronger roots, especially in dry spells and for newly planted trees.
  • Less effort. Automatic watering frees up staff and household time.

Practical water-saving tips

  • Water early in the morning or late in the evening to reduce evaporation.
  • Water deeply and less often rather than a little every day.
  • Spread mulch over beds and around trees to hold moisture in the soil.
  • Collect rainwater from roofs where you can, and use it for gardens.
  • Check pipes, joints and sprinkler heads regularly for leaks and blockages.

Irrigation works best when planned together with your planting. See our landscaping and tree planting services, or read about sustainable gardening practices.
