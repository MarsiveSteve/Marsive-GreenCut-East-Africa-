Trees that make spaces beautiful and healthier

Trees give shade, cool the air, protect soil, support birds and insects, and make a place feel finished. They also need the right start. Marsive GreenCare East Africa helps clients choose, plant and look after trees in corporate compounds, estates, institutions, urban spaces and reserve areas.

What we help with

  • Choosing species that suit your altitude, soil, space and purpose, whether shade, screening, flowers, fruit or habitat.
  • Planting of single trees, avenues, boundary lines and larger planting areas.
  • Aftercare advice: watering, mulching, staking and protection while young trees establish.
  • Green-space planting that combines trees with lawns, shrubs and ground cover. See landscaping.

Choosing the right tree

There is no single best tree for every site. Indigenous species that can suit many Kenyan highland and mid-altitude sites include Croton megalocarpus, Cordia africana, Markhamia lutea and wild olive (Olea africana), but suitability depends on altitude, rainfall and soil. Some species grow very large or have aggressive roots, so avoid planting them close to buildings, drains and boundary walls. Tell us about your site and we will recommend options.

How to give a tree the best start

  1. Plant at the start of the rains (in much of central Kenya, around March to May or October to December) so the roots establish naturally.
  2. Dig a wide hole, roughly twice the width of the root ball, and loosen the soil at the sides.
  3. Plant at the right depth with the root collar level with the ground, then firm the soil and water well.
  4. Mulch around the base, keeping it clear of the trunk, to hold moisture and suppress weeds.
  5. Water through dry spells for the first year or two, and protect young trees from livestock and damage.
