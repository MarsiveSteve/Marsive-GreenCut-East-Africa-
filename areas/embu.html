Landscaping, irrigation and tree planting in Embu County

Embu County stretches from the eastern slopes of Mount Kenya down into warmer, lower country, with Embu town as a growing centre for homes, institutions and businesses.

Marsive GreenCare East Africa works with homes and estates, schools and institutions, businesses and larger compounds across Embu, including Embu town, Runyenjes, Manyatta, Siakago and Kiritiri. Whether you need a lawn mowed, a garden redesigned, trees planted or an irrigation system installed, we can help.

What we offer in Embu

  • Landscaping and lawn establishment for homes, institutions and businesses
  • Shade trees and screening plants suited to warmer lower areas
  • Water-efficient irrigation to keep gardens green in dry periods
  • Regular mowing, trimming and green-space upkeep

Growing conditions in Embu

Conditions change from the cooler, wetter upper slopes to the hotter, drier lowlands, so the right planting plan depends on where your property sits.
