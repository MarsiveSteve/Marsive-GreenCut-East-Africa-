Landscaping, irrigation and tree planting in Kiambu County

Kiambu County wraps around Nairobi's northern edge, mixing growing residential estates, commercial developments, institutions and farmland. New and established properties alike benefit from well-planned green spaces.

Marsive GreenCare East Africa works with residential estates and new developments, institutions, business premises and large private compounds across Kiambu, including Kiambu, Thika, Ruiru, Juja, Kikuyu, Limuru and Githunguri. Whether you need a lawn mowed, a garden redesigned, trees planted or an irrigation system installed, we can help.

What we offer in Kiambu

  • Landscaping for new estates and developments that need established green space
  • Lawn care and land mowing for large compounds and open plots
  • Tree planting for shade, screening and boundary lines
  • Irrigation setups sized for large gardens and common areas

Growing conditions in Kiambu

Conditions vary across the county, from the cooler highlands around Limuru to the warmer areas toward Thika and Juja, so plant and grass choices should match the exact location.
