Landscaping, irrigation and tree planting in Kirinyaga County

Kirinyaga County runs from the slopes of Mount Kenya down to the hotter, drier Mwea plains, where irrigation is part of daily life. That range makes water-wise design especially valuable.

Marsive GreenCare East Africa works with homes, schools and institutions, businesses and larger rural compounds across Kirinyaga, including Kerugoya, Kutus, Sagana, Wanguru, Kagio and Mwea. Whether you need a lawn mowed, a garden redesigned, trees planted or an irrigation system installed, we can help.

What we offer in Kirinyaga

  • Irrigation design and installation, from drip lines to lawn sprinklers
  • Landscaping that matches the conditions at your altitude, from cool slopes to warm lowlands
  • Shade tree planting in hotter lower areas
  • Lawn mowing, hedge trimming and land care

Growing conditions in Kirinyaga

Temperatures and rainfall change a lot between the upper slopes and the Mwea lowlands, so we recommend plants, grasses and watering routines based on where your site is.
